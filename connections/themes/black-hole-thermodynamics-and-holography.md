# Black-hole thermodynamics, information and holography
> Curated theme · [Connections atlas](../../CONNECTIONS.md) · Kind: identity, duality

**The bridge.** Bekenstein argued that a black hole must carry entropy proportional to its horizon area, or the second law fails. Hawking then showed that quantum fields near a horizon radiate thermally, which made the analogy literal. *Established theory; unobserved for astrophysical black holes.* A solar-mass hole has $T_H\approx6.2\times10^{-8}$ K and $S\approx10^{77}k_B$.

Unruh found the same thermal effect for accelerated observers in flat space. Hawking's 1976 argument that evaporation destroys information turned the subject into a quantum-information problem: the Page curve shows how the entanglement entropy of the radiation must behave if evolution is unitary.

AdS/CFT made gravity in anti-de Sitter space exactly equivalent to a conformal field theory on its boundary. The Ryu–Takayanagi formula then made entanglement entropy a geometric area. Semiclassical "island" calculations (2019) reproduce the Page curve. *Current research.*

**Shared structure.**
$$T_H=\frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi GMk_B},\qquad S_{BH}=\frac{k_Bc^3A}{4G\hbar}=\frac{k_BA}{4\ell_P^2},\qquad T_U=\frac{\hbar a}{2\pi ck_B}$$
$$S_A=\frac{\operatorname{Area}(\gamma_A)}{4G_N}\ \ (\hbar=c=k_B=1),\qquad \lambda_L\le\frac{2\pi k_BT}{\hbar}$$
**Map.** Here $\kappa$ is the surface gravity, so $T=\hbar\kappa/(2\pi ck_B)$. Horizon area corresponds to entropy, and the boundary CFT state to bulk geometry. A minimal surface $\gamma_A$ corresponds to entanglement of region $A$, and black-hole formation to thermalization of the CFT.

**Where it breaks.**
- Our universe has $\Lambda>0$, not AdS. De Sitter holography is not established.
- Hawking radiation from known black holes is far below the CMB temperature, so the tests are indirect or come through analogues (see [analogue gravity](analogue-gravity.md)).
- The microscopic counting of $S_{BH}$ is achieved only in special string-theory settings.
