# C2021 · Hadronization as Unruh radiation: the registry formula is off by √(2π)

**Tier P (proof), status: correction confirmed.** This is not a new result. It is an error in the connection map that no reviewer caught, found by recomputing the claim.

**The registry says:** T = a/2π with a ~ √σ, giving T ≈ 160 MeV.

**Check:** with string tension σ = 0.16–0.20 GeV², a ~ √σ gives T = √σ/2π = **64–71 MeV**. That contradicts the record's own 160 MeV.

**The source** derives the deceleration from bringing a quark of transverse momentum k_T on-shell within the pair-fluctuation time Δτ ≃ 1/2k_T (its eqs. 57–58):

$$a \simeq 2k_T \simeq \sqrt{2\pi\sigma/c_0} \simeq 1\ \text{GeV}, \qquad T_Q = \frac{a}{2\pi} \simeq \sqrt{\frac{\sigma}{2\pi c_0}} \simeq 160\text{–}180\ \text{MeV}\quad (c_0 \simeq 1).$$

- sympy confirms T_paper/T_registry = √(2π/c₀) ≈ 2.51.
- σ = 0.16 GeV² gives 160 MeV; σ = 0.20 GeV² gives 178 MeV.
- Units: natural units (ħ = c = k_B = 1). σ in GeV² gives T in GeV; the SI form is T = ħa/(2πck_B).

**Fix for the record:** "…with T = a/2π and a ≃ √(2πσ), so T ≃ √(σ/2π) ≈ 160–180 MeV." Confidence stays *proposed*: the Unruh reading of hadronization is a conjecture of the source paper itself.

**Source:** Castorina, Kharzeev & Satz (2007). "Thermal hadronization and Hawking–Unruh radiation in QCD". *Eur. Phys. J. C* 52, 187. [arXiv:0704.1426](https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.1426)
