## Curated themes

- [Analogue gravity — horizons in fluids and condensates](connections/themes/analogue-gravity.md) — Sound in a flowing fluid propagates on an effective curved spacetime; where the flow outruns sound there is a horizon that should emit thermal phonons — a laboratory test of the kinematics behind Hawking radiation. *(fields 05, 04, 01, 03)*
- [Black-hole thermodynamics, information and holography](connections/themes/black-hole-thermodynamics-and-holography.md) — Black holes have a temperature and an entropy, which puts G, ħ, c and k_B in one formula and turns gravity into a problem of statistical mechanics and quantum information — culminating in holography, where spacetime geometry encodes entanglement. *(fields 05, 03, 04, 06, 08)*
- [Neutron stars — where six branches meet](connections/themes/neutron-stars-as-a-meeting-point.md) — A neutron star cannot be described by any one branch — general relativity sets its structure, nuclear and particle physics its equation of state, condensed matter its crust and superfluids, plasma physics its magnetosphere, and astrophysics and gravitational waves are how we measure it. *(fields 07, 05, 06, 08, 09, 10)*
- [Pairing across scales — metals, nuclei, neutron stars and cold atoms](connections/themes/pairing-across-scales.md) — BCS pairing is a property of any degenerate Fermi system with an attraction near its Fermi surface; the same gap equation describes superconductors (meV), atomic nuclei and neutron-star matter (MeV) and ultracold Fermi gases (nK). *(fields 08, 07, 04, 09, 03)*
- [Path integrals — quantum amplitudes and partition functions](connections/themes/path-integrals-quantum-and-statistical.md) — Rotating time to imaginary values turns Feynman's sum over histories into a Boltzmann-weighted partition function, so quantum field theory in D dimensions becomes classical statistical mechanics in D dimensions — the basis of lattice QCD, quantum Monte Carlo and instanton physics. *(fields 04, 03, 06, 00)*
- [The renormalization group and universality](connections/themes/renormalization-group-and-universality.md) — One idea for two fields — couplings that run with energy in quantum field theory, and critical exponents shared by unrelated systems in statistical mechanics — joined exactly by the Euclidean field theory ↔ statistical model correspondence. *(fields 03, 06, 08, 07, 00)*
- [Symmetry breaking and the Higgs–Anderson mechanism](connections/themes/symmetry-breaking-and-the-higgs-anderson-mechanism.md) — A superconductor and the electroweak vacuum are the same kind of state — a condensate that hides a gauge symmetry, gives the gauge field a mass and turns the would-be Goldstone mode into its longitudinal polarization. *(fields 00, 03, 06, 08)*
- [Geometric phases, gauge fields and band topology](connections/themes/topology-and-geometric-phases.md) — The Berry phase of a slowly transported quantum state has the mathematics of a gauge field; integrated over a crystal's Brillouin zone it becomes a quantized Chern number — the quantum Hall conductance — and its monopoles are Weyl points. *(fields 04, 08, 02, 06, 01)*
