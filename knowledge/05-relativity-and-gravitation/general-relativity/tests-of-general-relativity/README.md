# Tests of general relativity
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [General relativity](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Mercury perihelion (43″/century), light deflection (1.75″ at the solar limb), gravitational redshift (Pound–Rebka, GPS, optical clocks), Shapiro delay (Cassini $|\gamma-1|<2.3\times10^{-5}$), PPN formalism, weak equivalence principle (Eötvös, MICROSCOPE $\eta<10^{-15}$), lunar laser ranging, frame dragging (Gravity Probe B, LAGEOS), binary-pulsar orbital decay, GW tests of strong-field GR.

**Core ideas.**
- PPN parameters $\gamma,\beta$ are both 1 in GR; solar-system tests pin them to ~$10^{-5}$.
- The Hulse–Taylor binary's orbital decay matches GR's quadrupole radiation formula to ~0.2%.
- GPS must correct ~38 μs/day of relativistic clock offsets.

**Key equations.**
$$\Delta\phi=\frac{6\pi GM}{c^2a(1-e^2)},\qquad \delta\theta=\frac{1+\gamma}{2}\frac{4GM}{c^2b},\qquad \frac{\Delta\nu}{\nu}=\frac{gh}{c^2}$$

**Landmarks.** Pound & Rebka (1960); Shapiro (1964); Hulse & Taylor (1975); Everitt et al. (2011); Will (2014) — see [subfield landmarks](../README.md).
