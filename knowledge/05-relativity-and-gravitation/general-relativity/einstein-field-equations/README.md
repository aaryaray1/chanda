# Einstein field equations
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [General relativity](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Equivalence principle, Riemann/Ricci/Einstein tensors, Bianchi identities and $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0$, perfect-fluid stress–energy, cosmological constant, Einstein–Hilbert action and its variation, linearized gravity, Newtonian limit, energy conditions, ADM (3+1) formulation, initial-value problem.

**Core ideas.**
- 10 coupled nonlinear PDEs for the metric; the Bianchi identity guarantees local energy–momentum conservation.
- Weak fields: $g_{00}\approx-(1+2\Phi/c^2)$ recovers $\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho$.
- Gravity's energy cannot be localized (no gauge-invariant local energy density of the gravitational field).

**Key equations.**
$$R^\rho{}_{\sigma\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\Gamma^\rho_{\nu\sigma}-\partial_\nu\Gamma^\rho_{\mu\sigma}+\Gamma^\rho_{\mu\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda_{\nu\sigma}-\Gamma^\rho_{\nu\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\sigma},\qquad T^{\mu\nu}=(\rho+p/c^2)u^\mu u^\nu+pg^{\mu\nu}$$
