# Exact solutions
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [General relativity](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Birkhoff's theorem, Schwarzschild interior and exterior, Reissner–Nordström, Kerr and Kerr–Newman, no-hair theorem, FLRW cosmologies, de Sitter and anti-de Sitter, Gödel universe and closed timelike curves, pp-waves, Morris–Thorne wormholes and exotic matter, Petrov classification.

**Core ideas.**
- Symmetry (staticity, spherical/axial symmetry, homogeneity) reduces Einstein's equations to ODEs.
- Birkhoff: any spherically symmetric vacuum solution is Schwarzschild — no monopole gravitational radiation.
- Traversable wormholes require energy-condition violation.

**Key equations.**
$$ds^2=-\left(1-\frac{r_s}{r}\right)c^2dt^2+\frac{dr^2}{1-r_s/r}+r^2d\Omega^2,\ r_s=\frac{2GM}{c^2};\qquad ds^2_{\text{dS}}=-dt^2+e^{2Ht}d\mathbf x^2$$

**Landmark.** Kerr (1963) — see [subfield landmarks](../README.md).
