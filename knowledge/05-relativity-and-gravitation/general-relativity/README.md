# General relativity

> Part of [Relativity and Gravitation](../README.md) · Key reviews: [papers.md](papers.md)

## Overview
"Spacetime tells matter how to move; matter tells spacetime how to curve" (Wheeler). The equivalence principle makes gravity locally indistinguishable from acceleration, so it is encoded in the metric. GR has passed every test from millimetre lab scales to binary pulsars and black-hole mergers; its puzzles are at the extremes — singularities, the quantum regime, and the dark sector of cosmology.
