# Modified gravity
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [General relativity](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Lovelock's theorem (why modifications need extra fields or dimensions), Brans–Dicke and scalar–tensor theories, f(R), Horndeski and beyond-Horndeski, massive gravity (dRGT) and the vDVZ discontinuity, screening (chameleon, Vainshtein, symmetron), DGP braneworld, constraints from GW170817's speed of gravity, cosmological tests.

**Core ideas.**
- In 4D, GR + Λ is the unique metric theory with second-order equations from a local action (Lovelock).
- Modifications that explain cosmic acceleration must hide in the Solar System (screening).
- GW170817 ruled out many Horndeski models predicting $c_{\text{gw}}\neq c$.

**Key equations.**
$$S=\frac{1}{16\pi G}\int\sqrt{-g}\,f(R)\,d^4x,\qquad \gamma_{\text{BD}}=\frac{1+\omega}{2+\omega}$$

**Landmark.** Sotiriou & Faraoni (2010). "f(R) theories of gravity". *Rev. Mod. Phys.* 82, 451. [arXiv:0805.1726](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.1726)
