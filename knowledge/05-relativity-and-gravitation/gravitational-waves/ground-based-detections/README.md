# Ground-based detections
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [Gravitational waves](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Michelson interferometer with Fabry–Pérot arms and power/signal recycling, strain sensitivity ~$10^{-23}/\sqrt{\text{Hz}}$, seismic, thermal and quantum (shot and radiation-pressure) noise, squeezed-light injection, matched filtering and parameter estimation, observing runs O1–O4, GWTC catalogues, binary neutron stars and neutron-star–black-hole mergers, GW tests of GR, standard sirens.

**Core ideas.**
- A 4 km arm changes length by ~$10^{-18}$ m — a thousandth of a proton radius.
- Matched filtering against waveform templates extracts signals buried in noise.
- A network of detectors localizes sources and resolves polarization.

**Key equations.**
$$\Delta L=hL,\qquad \rho^2=4\int\frac{|\tilde h(f)|^2}{S_n(f)}df\ (\text{optimal SNR})$$

**Landmarks.** GW150914 (2016); GW170817 (2017) — see [subfield landmarks](../README.md).
