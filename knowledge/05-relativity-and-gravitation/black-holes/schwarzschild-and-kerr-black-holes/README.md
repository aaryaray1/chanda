# Schwarzschild and Kerr black holes
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [Black holes](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Schwarzschild coordinates and the coordinate singularity at $r_s$, Eddington–Finkelstein and Kruskal coordinates, Penrose diagrams, geodesics and effective potentials, photon sphere at $1.5\,r_s$, ISCO, Kerr metric in Boyer–Lindquist form, ergosphere and frame dragging, Penrose process and superradiance, no-hair theorem, quasinormal modes.

**Core ideas.**
- The horizon is a null surface, not a physical barrier; the singularity at $r=0$ is real.
- Kerr: spin $a=J/Mc\le GM/c^2$; prograde ISCO shrinks to $GM/c^2$ for maximal spin, raising accretion efficiency to ~42%.
- Ringdown frequencies depend only on $M$ and $a$ — a test of no-hair.

**Key equations.**
$$r_\pm=\frac{GM}{c^2}\pm\sqrt{\left(\frac{GM}{c^2}\right)^2-a^2},\qquad \eta_{\text{acc}}=1-\sqrt{1-\frac{2}{3}\frac{r_s}{r_{\text{ISCO}}}}\approx5.7\%\ (a=0)$$
