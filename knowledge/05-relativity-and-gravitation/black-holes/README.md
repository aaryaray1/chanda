# Black holes

> Part of [Relativity and Gravitation](../README.md) · Key reviews: [papers.md](papers.md)

## Overview
Black holes are the simplest macroscopic objects: in GR they are fully described by mass, spin and charge. They are also where GR and quantum mechanics collide most sharply — Hawking showed they radiate thermally, implying an entropy proportional to horizon area and raising the question of whether information is lost. Astrophysically, they power quasars, merge as gravitational-wave sources, and have now been imaged.
