# Black-hole thermodynamics and Hawking radiation
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [Black holes](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Area theorem, four laws of black-hole mechanics, Bekenstein's generalized second law, Hawking's derivation via Bogoliubov transformations, greybody factors, evaporation time, negative heat capacity, Unruh effect connection, microscopic entropy counting (Strominger–Vafa), analogue Hawking radiation in BECs and fluids.

**Core ideas.**
- Surface gravity plays the role of temperature, horizon area the role of entropy.
- Quantum fields near a horizon produce thermal radiation at $T_H=\hbar\kappa/2\pi ck_B$.
- Evaporation time $\sim10^{67}(M/M_\odot)^3$ yr — astrophysical black holes are colder than the CMB and still growing.

**Key equations.**
$$dM=\frac{\kappa}{8\pi G}dA+\Omega_HdJ+\Phi_HdQ,\qquad t_{\text{evap}}\approx\frac{5120\pi G^2M^3}{\hbar c^4}$$

**Landmarks.** Bekenstein (1973); Hawking (1975) — see [subfield landmarks](../README.md).
