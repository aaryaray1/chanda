# Black-hole imaging and astrophysical black holes
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [Black holes](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Stellar-mass black holes in X-ray binaries (Cyg X-1), spin measurement via continuum fitting and reflection, the S-stars orbiting Sgr A* (Nobel 2020), very-long-baseline interferometry and the EHT, shadows of M87* and Sgr A*, polarization and magnetic fields, Blandford–Znajek jets, GRMHD simulations, intermediate-mass black holes.

**Core ideas.**
- The shadow diameter is ≈$2\sqrt{27}\,GM/c^2$ (≈5.2 $GM/c^2$ in radius), nearly independent of spin — a test of the Kerr metric.
- EHT resolution $\lambda/D\approx20\ \mu$as at 1.3 mm with Earth-size baselines.
- Magnetically arrested disks can extract spin energy to power jets.

**Key equations.**
$$\theta_{\text{sh}}\approx\frac{2\sqrt{27}GM}{c^2D}\approx40\ \mu\text{as}\ (\text{M87}^*:\ 6.5\times10^9M_\odot,\ 16.8\ \text{Mpc};\ \text{EHT ring }42\pm3\ \mu\text{as}),\qquad L_{\text{Edd}}=\frac{4\pi GMm_pc}{\sigma_T}\approx1.26\times10^{31}\frac{M}{M_\odot}\ \text{W}$$

**Landmarks.** EHT (2019, 2022) — see [subfield landmarks](../README.md).
