# Analogue gravity
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [Quantum gravity](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Systems whose small excitations obey a wave equation on an *effective* curved spacetime, set by the background medium rather than by gravity. It covers:
- **the media:** acoustic metrics in flowing fluids and Bose–Einstein condensates (BECs), optical horizons in fibres, and water-wave tanks;
- **the effects reproduced:** sonic horizons and analogue Hawking radiation (stimulated and spontaneous), analogue Unruh radiation, rotational superradiance, and particle creation in expanding-universe simulators;
- **solid-state anomalies:** Weyl-semimetal transport that probes the mixed axial–gravitational anomaly;
- **the limits:** analogues reproduce the *kinematics* of quantum fields in curved spacetime (the field theory on a given background) but not Einstein's *dynamics*.

**Core ideas.**
- **Sound in a moving fluid is a field on curved spacetime.** In an irrotational, inviscid, barotropic flow, perturbations of the velocity potential obey the Klein–Gordon equation of an acoustic metric. Where the flow becomes supersonic there is a sonic horizon that sound cannot cross upstream (Unruh 1981).
- **The Hawking effect is a property of horizons, not of gravity.** Mode conversion at any horizon gives a thermal flux at $T_H=\hbar\kappa/2\pi k_B$. The derivation is robust to the modified short-wavelength dispersion of real media, which makes analogue gravity a laboratory answer to the trans-Planckian problem (Barceló, Liberati & Visser review).
- **Experiments have moved from classical stimulated emission to quantum signals:**
  - stimulated emission: water waves (2011), optics (2019);
  - spontaneous emission with correlations between Hawking quanta and their partners: BECs. Steinhauer's 2016 claim of entanglement was questioned (Leonhardt 2018), and a 2019 follow-up measured a thermal spectrum at the predicted temperature. The quantum, entangled character is *current research*, not settled.
- **Thermal gradients can stand in for gravity in solids.** A temperature gradient couples like a gravitational potential (Luttinger's trick). In the Weyl semimetal NbP, a positive magnetothermoelectric conductance for ∇T ∥ B is the signature expected from the mixed axial–gravitational anomaly of Weyl fermions (Gooth et al. 2017, *current research*).

**Key equations.** The acoustic metric (signature $-,+,+,+$; $\rho_0$, $\mathbf v_0$ and $c_s$ are the background density, flow velocity and sound speed):
$$ds^2=\frac{\rho_0}{c_s}\Big[-\big(c_s^2-v_0^2\big)\,dt^2-2\,\mathbf v_0\!\cdot d\mathbf x\,dt+d\mathbf x\cdot d\mathbf x\Big],\qquad \frac{1}{\sqrt{-g}}\,\partial_\mu\!\big(\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\nu\phi\big)=0$$

The analogue Hawking temperature of a one-dimensional flow with a sonic horizon. Here $\kappa$ is the velocity gradient at the horizon, in s⁻¹; with constants from CODATA 2022, $\hbar/2\pi k_B=1.216\times10^{-12}$ K s:
$$T_H=\frac{\hbar\kappa}{2\pi k_B},\qquad \kappa=\left|\frac{\partial(v-c_s)}{\partial x}\right|_{v=c_s}\;\Rightarrow\; T_H\approx1.2\ \text{nK}\times\frac{\kappa}{10^{3}\ \text{s}^{-1}}$$

**Open questions.**
- Has spontaneous Hawking emission been shown to be *entangled*, beyond reasonable doubt? Independent BEC and optical replications are still needed.
- Backreaction: can an analogue capture how emission changes the background? That is a step towards the dynamics of evaporation.
- Which features of the information paradox, such as Page-curve-like correlations, have measurable analogues?
- How far does the "emergent spacetime" programme go? Do Lorentz invariance and gravity themselves emerge from an underlying medium, as superfluid ³He models suggest?
- Are the Weyl-semimetal anomaly signatures uniquely gravitational, or can ordinary band and phonon effects mimic them?
