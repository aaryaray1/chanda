# Loop quantum gravity
> [Relativity and Gravitation](../../README.md) › [Quantum gravity](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Ashtekar–Barbero connection variables, holonomies and fluxes, spin-network states, discrete spectra of area and volume, Hamiltonian constraint problem, spin-foam (EPRL) models, black-hole entropy and the Immirzi parameter, loop quantum cosmology and the bounce, semiclassical limit.

**Core ideas.**
- Background-independent quantization: no fixed spacetime metric is assumed.
- Geometry is quantized; area eigenvalues are $8\pi\gamma\ell_P^2\sum\sqrt{j(j+1)}$.
- LQC replaces the Big Bang singularity with a bounce — but connecting LQG to low-energy GR remains difficult.

**Key equations.**
$$A=8\pi\gamma\ell_P^2\sum_i\sqrt{j_i(j_i+1)},\qquad H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho\left(1-\frac{\rho}{\rho_c}\right)\ (\text{LQC})$$

**Landmarks.** Ashtekar (1986); Rovelli & Smolin (1995) — see [subfield landmarks](../README.md).
