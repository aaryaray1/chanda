> The medium induced energy loss spectrum of a high energy quark or gluon traversing a hot QCD medium of finite volume is studied. > This brief review summarizes the main experimental discoveries made at RHIC and then discusses their implications. ### 5. Radiative energy loss of high-energy quarks in finite size nuclear matter and quark - gluon plasma
- **Authors:** Zakharov, B.G. · **Year:** 1997 · **Venue:** JETP Lett. 65, 615 · **Citations:** 766

> The induced gluon radiation of a high energy quark in a finite-size QCD medium is studied. For a sufficiently energetic quark produced inside a medium we find the radiative energy loss $\Delta E_{q}\propto L~{2}$, where L is the distance passed by quark in the medium. It has a weak dependence on the initial quark energy $E_{q}$. The $L~{2}$ dependence turns to $L~{1}$ as the quark energy decreases. Numerical calculations are performed for a cold nuclear matter and a hot quark-gluon plasma. For a quark incident on a nucleus we predict $\Delta E_{q}\approx 0.1 E_{q}(L/10 fm)~{\beta}$, with $\beta$ close to unity.

### 7. Probing the Short-Distance Structure of the Quark-Gluon Plasma with Energy Correlators
- **Authors:** Yang, Zhong, He, Yayun, Moult, Ian et al. · **Year:** 2024 · **Venue:** Phys.Rev.Lett. 132, 011901 · **Citations:** 116
- **Links:** [arXiv:2310.01500](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01500) · [doi:10.1103/physrevlett.132.011901](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.132.011901)

> Energy-energy correlators (EECs) are promising observables to study the dynamics of jet evolution in the quark-gluon plasma (QGP) through its imprint on angular scales in the energy flux of final-state particles. We carry out the first complete calculation of EECs using realistic simulations of high-energy heavy-ion collisions and dissect the different dynamics underlying the final distribution through analyses of jet propagation in a uniform medium. The EECs of γ-jets in heavy-ion collisions are found to be enhanced by the medium response from elastic scatterings instead of induced gluon radiation at large angles. In the meantime, EECs are suppressed at small angles due to energy loss and transverse momentum broadening of jet shower partons. These modifications are further shown to be sensitive to the angular scale of the in-medium interaction, as characterized by the Debye screening mass. Experimental verification and measurement of such modifications will shed light on this scale and the short-distance structure of the QGP in heavy-ion collisions.

> We study the Hall viscosity of the quark gluon plasma (QGP) created in non-central heavy-ion collisions. ### 15. Heavy-quark thermalization in the quark-gluon plasma via a fractional Langevin equation
- **Authors:** Jai Prakash, Mohammad Yousuf Jamal, Shanshan Cao et al. · **Year:** 2026 · **Venue:** arXiv preprint [nucl-th]
- **Links:** [arXiv:2610.26086](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26086)

> The dynamics of heavy quarks in the quark-gluon plasma is commonly described within a standard Langevin equation framework. In this work, we extend the conventional framework by using a fractional Langevin equation based on the Caputo derivative of order $α$ with $1<α\leq 2$. We solve the fractional Langevin equation and derive the corresponding {\textit {fractional fluctuation-dissipation theorem}} for the heavy quark thermalization in the quark-gluon plasma medium. We calculate the time evolution of the mean-squared momentum, mean-squared displacement, momentum distribution, energy loss, longitudinal momentum loss, transverse momentum broadening, and the nuclear suppression factor of the heavy quarks. The mean-squared momentum thermalizes to the same value for all $α$ at late times, in agreement with the fractional fluctuation-dissipation theorem, and the nuclear suppression factor shows a systematic $α$-dependence. The energy loss of an energetic heavy quark grows strongly with $α$. The time evolution of the heavy quark momentum distribution provides additional sensitivity to the fractional dynamics of the heavy quarks in the QGP medium.
