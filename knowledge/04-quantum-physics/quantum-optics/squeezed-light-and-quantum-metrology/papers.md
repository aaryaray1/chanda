> Squeezed light is a key resource in quantum metrology and quantum information science. Here, we modulate the third-order nonlinear response of a dielectric with strong ultrafast laser fields to control squeezed light generation on attosecond time scales. By tuning a sub-cycle phase delay between the input femtosecond pulses, we switch the generated light between amplitude-squeezed and phase-squeezed states. > Learning the Hamiltonian governing a quantum system is a central task in quantum metrology, sensing, and device characterization.
