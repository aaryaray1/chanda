> The Drosophila larva, a soft-body animal, can bend its body and roll efficiently to escape danger. > The thermodynamic basis of classical mechanics is presented. In this framework, ideal Newtonian mechanics emerges as the zero-dissipation limit of a more general, dissipative theory. > How the local energy transfer across scales in turbulence is encoded in the instantaneous deformation of fluid elements remains a fundamental question.
