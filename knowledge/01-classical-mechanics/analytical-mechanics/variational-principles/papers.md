> Abstract The method of variational completion allows one to transform an (in principle, arbitrary) system of partial differential equations – based on an intuitive “educated guess” – into the Euler–Lagrange one attached to a Lagrangian, by adding a canonical correction term. Here, we extend this technique to theories that involve at least two sets of dynamical variables: we show that an educated guess of the field equations with respect to one of these sets of variables only is sufficient to variationally complete these equations and recover a Lagrangian for the full theory, up to boundary terms and terms that do not involve the respective variables.
