- **Links:** [arXiv:2607.26312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26312)

> This work presents the demonstration of a superconducting niobium nitride (NbN) nanowire surface acoustic wave transducer, enabling a cryogenic acoustic delay line in scandium aluminum nitride (ScAlN) on silicon carbide (SiC).
