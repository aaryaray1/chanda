> Discrepancies have emerged between the predictions of standard cold dark matter (CDM) theory and observations of clustering on sub-galactic scales. > We assess neutrino-assisted dark matter self-scattering in two scalar-mediator realizations by combining mediator-resolved potentials, numerical scattering calculations, thermal relic production, and the adopted observational constraints.
