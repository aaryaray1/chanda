> We study the vacuum structure and dyon spectrum of N =2 supersymmetric gauge theory in four dimensions, with gauge group SU(2). The theory turns out to have remarkably rich and physical properties which can nonetheless be described precisely; exact formulas can be obtained, for instance, for electron and dyon masses and the metric on the moduli space of vacua. > In quantum optics and related fields, cavity quantum electrodynamics provides a powerful framework for modeling the low-energy properties of bound charges coupled to electromagnetic fields, using a minimal set of atomic levels and isolated photonic modes. > We develop a bottom-up open effective field theory (EFT) for non-Abelian gauge theories within the Schwinger--Keldysh formalism.
