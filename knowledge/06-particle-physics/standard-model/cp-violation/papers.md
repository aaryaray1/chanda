### 10. CP violation of baryon decays with N π rescatterings*
- **Authors:** Wang, Jian-Peng, Yu, Fu-Sheng · **Year:** 2024 · **Venue:** Chin.Phys.C 48, 101002 · **Citations:** 26
- **Links:** [arXiv:2407.04110](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04110) · [doi:10.1088/1674-1137/ad75f4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-1137/ad75f4)

> There is a long-standing puzzle that the CP violation (CPV) in the baryon systems has never been well established in experiments, while the CPV of mesons have been observed by decades. > CP violation, which involves breaking the combined charge-conjugation (C) and parity (P) symmetries, is essential for understanding the observed matter-antimatter asymmetry in the universe. Despite the successes of the SM, the amount of CP violation it predicts is not enough to explain baryogenesis, prompting searches for new sources of CP violation in other areas of particle physics.
