# History and philosophy of physics

> Part of [Foundations](../README.md) · Key reviews: [papers.md](papers.md)

## Overview
History explains why physics has the concepts it has; philosophy examines what those concepts mean (space, time, probability, causation, laws, emergence) and how theories are confirmed. Physics education research applies the methods of physics to how physics is learned — and finds that interactive engagement beats lecturing.

## Topics
- [History of physics](history-of-physics/README.md) — from Galileo to the Standard Model and beyond.
- [Philosophy of physics](philosophy-of-physics/README.md) — interpretation of QM, spacetime, emergence, laws, explanation.
- [Physics education research](physics-education-research/README.md) — misconceptions, concept inventories, active learning.
