### 15. Green's function on the Tate curve
- **Authors:** An Huang, Rebecca Rohrlich, Yaojia Sun et al. · **Year:** 2025 · **Venue:** arXiv preprint [math.NT]
- **Links:** [arXiv:2512.24935](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24935)

> Motivated by the question of defining a $p$-adic string worldsheet action in genus one, we define a Laplacian operator on the Tate curve, and study its Green's function.
