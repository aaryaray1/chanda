> Despite a vigorous effort, a comprehensive and satisfactory understanding of the dynamics underlying the glass transition in finite-dimensional systems has not yet emerged. One key hallmark of supercooled liquids approaching the glass transition is the breakdown of the classical Stokes-Einstein (SE) relation, manifested as the decoupling between the self-diffusion coefficient and structural relaxation time, commonly described by a fractional SE relation. Whether and how the fractional SE exponents vary remains an ongoing debate, even for the simplest supercooled liquids. > Soft glassy materials display rich and complex flow behaviors across both macroscopic and molecular scales, and a fundamental understanding of these phenomena remains an outstanding challenge.
