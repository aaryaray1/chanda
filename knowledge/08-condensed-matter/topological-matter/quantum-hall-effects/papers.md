> We show that edge states in fractional quantum Hall effect samples can be selectively probed and excited with sub-terahertz optical spectroscopy. The CF edge-state absorption peaks appear in the millimeter-wave to sub-terahertz range, e.g., 60-500 GHz at B = 10 T in GaAs, i.e. they are blueshifted with respect to the bulk CF cyclotron frequency but are well below the integer quantum Hall cyclotron frequency scale at the same magnetic fields.
