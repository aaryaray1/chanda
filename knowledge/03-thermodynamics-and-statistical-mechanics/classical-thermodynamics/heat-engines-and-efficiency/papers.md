> Quantum heat engines require precise control over thermal reservoirs and the energies of the quantum working medium. - **Authors:** Shogo Toma, Atsushi Noguchi, Ken Funo et al. · **Year:** 2026 · **Venue:** arXiv preprint [quant-ph]
- **Links:** [arXiv:2607.08713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08713)

> Whether a heat engine can approach Carnot efficiency while maintaining finite power is a fundamental question in finite-time thermodynamics. For classical Markovian heat engines with local interactions, the power-efficiency trade-off forbids an asymptotic approach to Carnot efficiency at finite power. In quantum systems, by contrast, degeneracy, symmetry, and collective jumps have been theoretically predicted to enable such an asymptotic attainment by enhancing activity. It has remained open, however, whether this mechanism can be realized in an experimentally implementable heat engine. In this Letter, we propose a superconducting-circuit heat engine that emulates the collective enhancement, thereby enabling an asymptotic approach to Carnot efficiency at finite power. This result demonstrates that, in an implementable model, such an enhanced dissipative mechanism circumvents the power-efficiency trade-off of classical Markovian engines. Our work connects abstract bounds in finite-time thermodynamics to a concrete circuit-QED platform and suggests a route toward quantum-device design based on collectively enhanced dissipative processes.

### 12. Beyond low-dissipation regime: general condition for Curzon-Ahlborn efficiency
- **Authors:** Yuki Izumida · **Year:** 2026 · **Venue:** arXiv preprint [cond-mat.stat-mech]
- **Links:** [arXiv:2610.24434](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24434)

> In recent years, physicists have extensively investigated the universality of Curzon-Ahlborn (CA) efficiency as the efficiency at maximum power of heat engines, which, like Carnot efficiency, is determined only by heat-bath temperatures. While the universality of the CA efficiency to the first order in the temperature difference is established within linear response theory in the inverse operation time, it is generally lost at quadratic order. In the low-dissipation Carnot heat engine model known as the standard framework, the symmetric dissipation condition is necessary. Yet, it is not a necessary condition as the original CA model that adopts endoreversibility does not require such symmetry. In this study, we extend the low-dissipation model to include nonlinear response in the inverse operation time and derive a general condition under which the CA efficiency is recovered in the quadratic order. The generalized symmetric dissipation condition and the CA model with Newton's law of cooling are shown to be special cases of this condition. By applying the obtained condition, we also clarify the condition under which an endoreversible model with a general heat-transfer law exhibits the CA efficiency.

> Single-particle heat engines at atomic and colloidal scales obey the universal thermodynamic bounds on work and efficiency. ### 15. Holographic heat engines for Schwarzschild black holes

- **Links:** [arXiv:2607.03749](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03749)

> We construct reversible black hole heat engines in asymptotically flat spacetime using quasi-local gravitational thermodynamics. We enclose a Schwarzschild black hole in a finite spherical cavity and identify the working substance with the holographically dual thermal system on the cavity boundary. The surface pressure and the boundary area define a thermodynamic pressure-volume pair, while all coupling constants of the gravitational theory remain fixed.
