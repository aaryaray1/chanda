# Exactly solvable models
> [Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics](../../README.md) › [Equilibrium statistical mechanics](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** 1D Ising via transfer matrices, Onsager's 2D Ising solution, Yang–Baxter equation and integrability, six- and eight-vertex models, Bethe ansatz for the Heisenberg chain, Lieb–Liniger Bose gas, Tonks–Girardeau gas, free-fermion mappings (Jordan–Wigner).

**Core ideas.**
- Transfer matrices turn partition functions into eigenvalue problems: $Z=\mathrm{Tr}\,T^N\approx\lambda_{\max}^N$.
- 1D systems with short-range interactions have no finite-temperature phase transitions.
- Integrable models have extensively many conserved quantities — they don't thermalize conventionally (GGE).

**Key equations.**
$$\sinh\frac{2J}{k_BT_c}=1\Rightarrow k_BT_c=\frac{2J}{\ln(1+\sqrt2)}\approx2.269J\ (\text{2D Ising})$$

**Landmarks.** Bethe (1931); Lieb & Liniger (1963) — see [subfield landmarks](../README.md); Onsager (1944) — see [phase transitions](../../phase-transitions-and-critical-phenomena/README.md).
