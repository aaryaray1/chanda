# Critical exponents and universality
> [Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics](../../README.md) › [Phase transitions](../README.md) · Literature: [papers.md](papers.md)

**Scope.** Definitions of $\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta,\nu,\eta$, scaling and hyperscaling relations, Widom scaling hypothesis, finite-size scaling, universality classes (Ising, XY, Heisenberg, percolation, directed percolation, KPZ), experimental tests (liquid–gas, superfluid helium in space), conformal-bootstrap determinations.

**Core ideas.**
- Exponents depend only on dimension, order-parameter symmetry and interaction range.
- Two exponents determine the rest via scaling relations.
- 3D Ising: $\nu\approx0.62999$, $\eta\approx0.03630$ (bootstrap); liquid–gas critical points share these values.

**Key equations.**
$$\xi\sim|t|^{-\nu},\quad C\sim|t|^{-\alpha},\quad M\sim(-t)^\beta,\quad \chi\sim|t|^{-\gamma},\quad G(r)\sim r^{-(d-2+\eta)}$$
