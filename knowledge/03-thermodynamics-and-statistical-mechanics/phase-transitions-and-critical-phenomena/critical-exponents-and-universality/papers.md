### 14. Dynamical Critical Properties of the Random-Bond three-state Potts Model
- **Authors:** Zeynep Demir Vatansever, Ulvi Kanbur, Mohammed Sadra Najafi et al. · **Year:** 2026 · **Venue:** Physical Review E, 114, 034102 (2026)

> We study the dynamic critical behavior of the two-dimensional random-bond three-state Potts model using large-scale Monte Carlo simulations with the Wolff and Swendsen--Wang cluster algorithms. At the disorder-dependent critical temperature, we compute integrated and exponential autocorrelation times and extract the dynamic critical exponent $z$ via finite-size scaling. Critical slowing down is significantly weakened by bond randomness, with the dynamic exponent decreasing from $z \approx 0.54$ in the pure system to $z \approx 0.24$ at strong disorder. > We determine the critical exponents $η$, $ν$, and the correction-to-scaling exponent $ω$ of the three-dimensional Ising universality class by resumming the recently computed seven- and eight-loop renormalization-group series in the $ε=4-d$ expansion (O.~Schnetz, \textit{Phys.
